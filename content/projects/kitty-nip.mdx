## The Problem

Kitty Nip is a cat dating app on Android and iOS with 10K+ downloads on Google Play. The app predates my work. When the previous team left, it was crashing on newer Android devices after native platform changes, and nobody owned the backend.

## What I Built

As lead engineer I took over the whole product, backend included.

- **Kept it running on current Android.** Upgraded React Native to **0.79** and its libraries onto the **New Architecture** (Fabric and TurboModules), handled Android's **16 KB page size**, and converted legacy class components to functional components.
- **Sign-in.** Shipped Google, Apple and phone sign-in.
- **Subscriptions.** Reworked the plans with restore purchase, fixed iOS and Android subscription issues, and added a **Cloud Function** that validates Android purchase receipts against the Play Developer API.
- **Photos and premium.** The Photos screen ties the plan to adding photos of the owner as well as the cat, and a Cloud Function I added runs **Google Vision** label and face detection on uploads.
- **Swipe to conversation.** The swipe card opens a full profile with bio, distance and every photo; new matches sit above the message list, unread chats are marked, and photos send inline.

Live on [Google Play](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=computer.star.kittynip) and the [App Store](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/kitty-nip-cat-dating-app/id1520805359).

## Key Challenges

Taking over a live app meant fixing what users were already hitting before adding anything. The crashes came from native changes in Android upgrades, so the fix was the platform upgrade itself rather than patches on top of an old React Native version.

## Results

The app now reports **100% crash-free** on Crashlytics, with **10K+ downloads** on Google Play and a live App Store listing, under one engineer's ownership.

## Stack Decisions

The React Native and Firebase stack was kept rather than rewritten: a live social app does not justify rewrite risk. Upgrading in place to React Native 0.79 and the New Architecture fixed the crashes and leaves the app on a supported path.
