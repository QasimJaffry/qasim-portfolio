## The Problem

Local businesses, from hair salons to caterers and home-made product sellers, needed one place where nearby customers could find them by category and buy discount vouchers, with a separate way in for the business owners themselves.

## What I Built

At StackUp Solutions I **led the architecture and delivery of Blackpin Business with a team of four**, working hands-on in the code and running the client meetings.

- **Two roles at sign-in.** Business owners and users each choose their own way into the app.
- **Browse by category.** A home screen of categories such as products, catering, hair salons, beauty and wellness, and home-made products, with search.
- **Map or grid.** Each category opens on a map with pins and featured businesses, or as a grid of business cards showing how many vouchers are left.
- **Business profiles.** Info, vouchers, reviews and location tabs, with a photo gallery and links to the business's website and social pages.
- **Vouchers and checkout.** Coupon-style vouchers with a price, expiry date and stock left, bought through a card checkout. Cards can be saved and managed from the account tab.
- **Chat and favourites** sit in the bottom tabs next to home and account.

## Key Challenges

**Two products in one app.** Owners and customers see different things, so the role choice at sign-in had to set up the whole experience behind it.

**Vouchers with limited stock.** Each voucher shows how many are left and when it expires, so the listing, the profile and the checkout all had to agree on the same numbers.

**Leading while building.** Setting the architecture and code review standards for a team of four while still shipping screens myself.

## Results

Delivered to the client as a mobile app for business owners and their customers, with category and map search, vouchers and card checkout.

## Stack Decisions

React Native for iOS and Android from one codebase, with a map view for local search and an in-app card checkout with saved cards.
