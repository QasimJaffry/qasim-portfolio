## The Problem

Home owners with small jobs, such as a leaking tap or a room to paint, had no simple way to post the job, compare quotes from contractors and agree a visit.

## What I Built

At StackUp Solutions I worked on **Zitro Workplace**, a two-sided jobs app for Zitro Services LLC.

- **Post a job.** Title, category, location, description, date and time.
- **Track jobs.** Posted jobs and invites on the home screen; active jobs, proposals and past jobs under My Jobs.
- **Proposals.** Each contractor's proposal shows their rating, hourly rate, the time they need, the visit date and time, and a written quote.
- **Hire or chat.** The owner can hire straight from the proposal or open a chat with the contractor to agree the price first.
- **Messages and notifications** in their own tabs.

## Key Challenges

**One job, two sides.** The same job moves from posted to proposals to active to past, and both the owner and the contractor have to see the same state at each step.

**Quotes people can compare.** Rate, hours, visit time and the written quote sit on one screen, so an owner can decide without going back and forth in chat.

## Results

Shipped to the client as part of StackUp Solutions' client work.

## Stack Decisions

React Native for iOS and Android from one codebase. Chat sits next to the proposal rather than replacing it, so the agreed terms stay on record in the job.
