## The Problem

In Pakistan many discounts come from two places at once: brands run seasonal sales, and banks give extra discounts on their debit and credit cards at certain stores on certain days. Shoppers rarely see both, so they miss offers they already qualify for.

## What I Built

At StackUp Solutions I worked on **Yam Rewards**, a deals app built for a client.

- **Onboarding** that explains the app: get news of the latest sales before stock runs out.
- **Home feed** of brand sales with a promo carousel and categories such as clothing, footwear and food, each brand showing its current discount.
- **Discounts by bank.** Search for your bank and see what its cards unlock.
- **Offer pages** listing each card's discount, the days and stores where it applies, and its terms, with share and favourite buttons.

## Key Challenges

**Two kinds of offer in one place.** A brand sale and a bank card discount work differently, so the app keeps them on separate tabs and joins them on the offer page.

**Offers with conditions.** A card discount may only apply on some days or in some stores, so those conditions had to be visible before someone goes shopping.
