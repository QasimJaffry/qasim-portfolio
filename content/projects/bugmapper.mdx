## The Problem

[BugMapper](https://www.bugmapper.com.tr/) is a precision pest management platform for commercial greenhouses, deployed in Kayseri, Türkiye. Staff walk the greenhouse, check insect traps, record counts and log treatments.

Done by hand, counting a trap takes minutes, and the signal inside a greenhouse comes and goes. The work can't stop when the connection does, and nothing recorded offline can be lost.

## What I Built

I have been a contract engineer on BugMapper since September 2023, delivered through Jafrix Systems since 2025. I built the **React Native (Expo)** field app and contributed to the web dashboard where administrators review organisations and their deployments.

- **QR scanning.** Scan a trap or location QR code with the phone camera to open its record: crop, trap type, field and past counts.
- **Trap list.** Summary tiles for traps to count, ending soon and not counted yet, with traps near end of life flagged in red.
- **Counts and observations.** Record trap counts and plant observations by sector, tunnel and row, then collect or finalise a trap on the same screen.
- **Treatments.** Validated chemical-entry forms and an applications history per product and field.
- **Offline queue.** Deployments and photos go into a local **SQLite** queue first and sync when the connection returns. Pending, in-progress and failed items are tracked separately, and staff can sync one item or all of them.
- **Files from the greenhouse.** Encrypted file transfer between the app and an on-site device, with integrity checks, then upload to the cloud.
- **English and Turkish**, with drawer navigation.

## Key Challenges

**Offline has to feel normal.** Every count lands on the phone first, and a Pending Sync screen shows what is waiting, so a greenhouse with no signal loses nothing.

**Failed uploads must stay visible.** Trap actions and device files sync on separate paths. A failed photo upload never looks like a failed trap count, and failed items wait to be retried instead of disappearing.

**Testing without the hardware.** The on-site device is not always on hand, so I built a mock server for the file transfer, which lets the transfer and its integrity checks be tested without it.

## Results

In production in Kayseri, Türkiye. The client reports that recording a trap now takes about **10 seconds**, against **5 to 10 minutes** for a manual count. The platform has been covered in the agricultural trade press, including [Daily Sabah](https://www.dailysabah.com/turkiye/ai-improves-pest-detection-in-kayseris-soilless-farming/news).

## Stack Decisions

React Native with Expo and TypeScript for the field app. **SQLite** for the offline queue, so counts and photos stay on the phone until they sync. **Zustand** for app state, **Firebase Authentication** for sign-in and **i18next** for English and Turkish.
