## The Problem

A creator marketplace is two products at once. Brands need to create campaigns, review applicants and see what they are paying. Creators need onboarding, linked social accounts, listings, a portfolio and a wallet. The hard part is bringing **social account data** and **payments** into one place both sides can trust.

## What I Built

At StackUp Solutions I worked on the **frontend** of **Snapwork**, on both the **React Native** app and the **React** web app, with teammates on the backend. My part covered the product screens, the **Facebook and TikTok API** integrations that pull in account data, and payments through **MangoPay**.

- **Linked accounts.** An onboarding step to link TikTok, Instagram, YouTube, Facebook and Twitter, so account data can be used in matching and applications.
- **Campaign listings.** Creators browse brand campaigns with platform requirements, follower minimums and price or discount, with the wallet in the navigation.
- **Applicants.** Brands see each applicant's followers, engagement rate, rating and receipt value in one place.
- **Brand dashboard.** Sales, discounts, platform fees, users and an overview of campaigns.
- **Portfolio.** A grid of a creator's images and videos.
- **Publishing a campaign.** Brief, targeting, interests, locations and a cost and margin breakdown before it goes live.

## Key Challenges

**Two sides, one system.** The brand web app and the creator mobile app had very different jobs but had to feel like one product.

**Social data in the product.** Linking an account is more than signing in: the app has to explain the permissions, then show followers, engagement and eligibility in campaign and applicant views.

**Clear money.** Price, discount, fees, receipt value and wallet had to be easy to read, so creators and brands knew what was paid and why.

## Results

Shipped as a client product on mobile and web, built across 2022 and 2023. It has since been taken offline.

## Stack Decisions

React Native for the creator app and React for the brand web app. MangoPay for the wallet and payouts. The Facebook and TikTok APIs pull account data into onboarding, listings and applicant tables.
