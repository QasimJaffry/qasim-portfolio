## The Problem

Market research usually means many tabs and queries. Agenticly puts one assistant on web, Android and iOS: ask about crypto and stocks in plain language and get a table, a chart and a written answer back. Some research repeats every week, so agents can also run on a schedule, and running them is a paid feature that has to behave the same on every device.

## What I Built

I led the engineering on this team product at Star Computer Labs. I built and own:

- The **React Native** app and the **Next.js** web client, sharing one **Firebase** backend, so each agent's status, Run Now and run history appear on both.
- **Scheduled agents** on Firebase Functions: write the question, pick frequency and time, pause without deleting. Each run saves its own dated result, so runs can be compared.
- **Payments**: sign-in with email, Google or Apple, a 7-day free trial, **RevenueCat** on mobile and **Stripe** on web, both writing one subscription record that decides who can run an agent.
- The **landing page**, with App Store and Google Play links, free sign-up, and navigation to pricing, market trends and the blog.

The research backend that answers the questions (Python, FastAPI, LangGraph) was built by teammates.

Live on [Google Play](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=computer.star.agenticly), the [App Store](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/agenticly-stock-crypto-ai/id6760048191) and [agenticly.app](https://agenticly.app).

## Key Challenges

**One plan, two stores and the web.** A customer can pay through the App Store, Google Play or Stripe. Every path writes the same subscription record, so access never depends on where someone paid.

**Answers people can read at a glance.** A typed question replaces a dozen research tabs, so every answer comes back as a table, a chart and plain text rather than a query language to learn.

**Repeat research without repeat work.** Scheduled runs keep their own history, so a weekly question builds a record instead of overwriting the last answer.

## Results

**26K installs** across web, Android and iOS combined, about **2.3K monthly active users**, and typically **100% crash-free** on Firebase Crashlytics. Rated 4.5 on Google Play.

## Stack Decisions

React Native for both stores and Next.js for the web, on one Firebase backend with Cloud Functions for scheduled agents. RevenueCat absorbs App Store and Play receipt handling; Stripe handles web trials and cards; both normalise into one subscription record the clients read.
