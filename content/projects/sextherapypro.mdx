## The Problem

Sexual wellness apps often feel clinical or gimmicky. People want a private space that listens without judgement, turns their goals into a daily plan, and offers exercises that feel human rather than a generic chatbot.

## What I Built

TherapyPro is an **Expo / React Native** app for an AI sexual-wellness companion, built at StackUp Solutions. I built two parts of it:

- **Subscriptions.** The **RevenueCat** subscription flow.
- **AI chat.** The in-app chat with **Eros**, the AI guide, on **AWS Bedrock**: a short intake ("Improve desire and arousal"), follow-up questions, an open "Ask me anything" mode, and an Analyze action that turns a conversation into guidance.

The rest of the app sits around that chat:

- **Personalised plan.** Daily tasks such as sensate touch, desire mapping and homework, with progress on the home screen.
- **Topics and sessions.** Pick a theme (attachment, boundaries, emotional expression) and return to recent AI sessions.
- **Onboarding goals.** Choose how you want to feel after using the app: connected, more knowledgeable, less ashamed.
- **Guided exercises.** A body map, desire mapping and timed couple games such as **The 3 Minute Game**.

## Key Challenges

**A chat that works like a session.** Intake, Analyze and recent sessions had to feel like steps with a therapist rather than an empty text box, with Bedrock responses shown in a calm conversation screen.

**A sensitive subject.** The design had to invite trust without feeling medical or casual, while keeping plan, chat and exercises easy to find.
## Results

An Expo app with personalised plans, chat with Eros on AWS Bedrock, guided exercises and RevenueCat subscriptions.

## Stack Decisions

Expo and React Native for iOS and Android from one codebase. AWS Bedrock runs the Eros chat and Analyze on a managed model service. RevenueCat handles store subscriptions on both platforms.
