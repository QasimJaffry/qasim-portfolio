## The Problem

Sexual wellness apps often feel clinical or gimmicky. People need a private space that can listen without judgment, turn goals into a day-to-day plan, and offer exercises that feel human — not a generic chatbot with a lavender theme.

## What I Built

I owned the **Expo / React Native frontend** for **Sex Therapy Pro**: an AI sexual-wellness companion with a personalized daily plan, chat with **Eros** (AI therapist powered by **AWS Bedrock**), interactive desire/body mapping, and couple-oriented exercises such as **The 3 Minute Game**.

## Major Features

- **Personalized plan** — Daily task progress (e.g. sensate touch, desire mapping, homework) with a calm home dashboard and bottom nav.
- **Chat with Eros** — Conversational intake (“Improve desire/arousal”), follow-ups, free-form “Ask me anything,” and an Analyze action to turn chat into guidance via Bedrock.
- **Topic & session hub** — Choose therapy themes (attachment, boundaries, emotional expression) and revisit recent AI sessions.
- **Onboarding goals** — Bubble selection for how you want to feel after using the app (connected, knowledgeable, less ashamed, etc.).
- **Guided exercises** — Sensory foreplay body map, desire mapping, and timed couple games introduced with clear start screens.

## Key Challenges

**Sensitive UX without feeling medical or casual.** Soft gradients, illustration, and copy had to invite trust while keeping hierarchy clear for plan → chat → exercise.

**AI chat as product, not a text box.** Intake, Analyze, and recent sessions needed to feel like a therapist workflow, with Bedrock responses wired into a calm mobile conversation UI.

**Interactive tools on mobile.** Body-map selection and multi-select goal bubbles had to stay readable and tappable without cluttering the pastel layout.

## Results

Sex Therapy Pro shipped as a mobile AI wellness frontend: personalized plans, Eros chat, and guided intimacy exercises in one Expo client.

## Stack Decisions

**Expo / React Native** for iOS and Android from one codebase. **AWS Bedrock** powers Eros and Analyze so conversational guidance stays on a managed model path; the plan and exercise screens turn that output into concrete daily actions instead of leaving users in an endless chat thread.
