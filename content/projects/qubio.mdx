## The Problem

Qubio Global, a client in Australia, needed digital identity that lives on **QR and NFC** tags: pages people open with a scan or a tap, which the owner can change at any time from a phone. Behind that sat a web dashboard, analytics and paid plans.

## What I Built

At StackUp Solutions I worked on the **Qubio Ecosystem** for **18 months**. Users build content stacks of video, audio, images and links and share them as QR codes that keep working when the content changes. I led the frontend and the payments:

- The **React Native** app for registering tags and editing page content on the phone.
- The **Next.js** web dashboard, with **D3.js** analytics, **Redux Toolkit** and **RTK Query**.
- **RevenueCat** subscriptions.

I led a team of two, a senior frontend developer and a junior backend developer working in MongoDB, and ran daily scrums with the client's team in Australia.

## Key Challenges

**Two clients, one backend.** The app and the dashboard read and write the same data, so changes on one side could not break the other over 18 months of releases.

**Working across time zones.** Daily scrums with the Australian team kept a small team in Pakistan in step with the client's priorities.

## Results

Delivered the app, the web dashboard and subscriptions for Qubio Global over the 18-month engagement.

## Stack Decisions

React Native for editing on the phone and Next.js for the denser dashboard work, with D3.js for analytics charts. Redux Toolkit and RTK Query kept the dashboard's data fetching and caching in one place, and RevenueCat handled store billing.
